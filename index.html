ABOUT BROWNIES

A brownie is a rich, chocolate-flavoured baked dessert that is usually cut into square or rectangular pieces. It has a delicious chocolate taste and can have a cakey or chewy texture. Brownies are famous all around the world and are loved by chocolate lovers everywhere.

MAIN INGREDIENTS

  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Cocoa powder
  • Chocolate
  • Butter
  • Vanilla essence

MY FAVOURITE BROWNIE RECIPE

Here is a simple way to make delicious brownies at home!

  1. Melt chocolate and butter together.
  2. Mix eggs and sugar in another bowl.
  3. Add the melted chocolate mixture.
  4. Add flour and cocoa powder.
  5. Mix everything until smooth.
  6. Pour the mixture into a baking tray.
  7. Bake at 180°C for about 20-25 minutes.
  8. Let it cool and cut into square pieces.
